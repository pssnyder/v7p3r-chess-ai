│ WHY THIS MATTERS:                                                           │

│   • A model that says "this is a good move" (even off by 1 grade) is useful │
│   • A model that says "this is the 6th best" when it's 5th is useless       │
│                                                                              │
│ RESULT:                                                                     │
│   Training optimizes for what matters: move quality bias, not label         │
│   precision                                                                 │
